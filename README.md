### Anzhella Pankratova

Founder of **Corypheus Studio** — a platform for automated AI photoshoots for fashion and product brands.

Garments in, a finished lookbook out: one model, one light, the same face and an accurate garment in every shot. No operator in the loop — every frame is checked automatically, and the ones that fail are regenerated.

I've worked on image generation since the first open diffusion models: I co-built the first LoRA, ControlNet and IP-Adapter training for **FLUX.1** ([x-flux](https://github.com/XLabs-AI/x-flux), 2k+ stars) and have shipped photoshoot engines for a European fashion brand and a product photography studio.
